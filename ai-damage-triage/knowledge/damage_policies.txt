PRODUCT DAMAGE HANDLING POLICIES

Screen Damage:
Screen cracks, shattered displays, dead pixels, or display failures should be assigned to the Hardware Team.

Battery Damage:
Battery swelling, leakage, puncture, overheating, or other battery-related damage must be assigned to the Safety Team. Battery safety issues are high priority.

Water Damage:
Products showing liquid exposure, water ingress, corrosion caused by liquid, or moisture damage should be assigned to the Hardware Team.

Packaging Damage:
Crushed boxes, torn packaging, damaged shipping containers, or packaging-only damage should be assigned to the Logistics Team.

Cosmetic Damage:
Scratches, dents, discoloration, minor surface damage, or appearance-related issues should be assigned to the Quality Team.

Breakage:
Broken ceramic, glass, plastic, or other physically fragmented products should be assigned to the Quality Team. If the product is unusable, severity should normally be HIGH.

General Rule:
If the damage type does not clearly match an existing category, assign the case to the Review Team for manual investigation.
